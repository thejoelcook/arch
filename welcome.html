Get set up

  1. 1

    Open the sidebar

    Click the Arch icon in your toolbar any time, or press . Tip: pin Arch from the puzzle-piece menu so it's always there.

  2. 2

    Use ⌘S to show and hide it, like Arc

    Chrome only lets you set this yourself. On the shortcuts page, find Arch, click the pencil next to Toggle the Arch sidebar, and press ⌘S.

  3. 3

    Move it to the left

    In Chrome Settings › Appearance, set Side panel position to Left, just like Arc.

  4. 4

    Bring your Arc setup

    Import your spaces, pinned tabs, folders and favorites in about a minute. Arc doesn't need to be running.

What Arch can do

Private by design. Arch has no accounts, servers or tracking. Everything stays in Chrome on your computer, plus your own Chrome sync if you turn it on.
